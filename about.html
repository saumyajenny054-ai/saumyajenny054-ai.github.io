Welcome to LumiNest.

LumiNest by Mayumi is a small digital-product brand focused on creating simple, practical resources for modern everyday life.

From planners and printable tools to creative templates, each product is designed to be easy to understand, pleasant to use, and ready when you need it.

This is a growing little nest of digital resources — created with care, curiosity, and the belief that useful things can also be beautiful.
